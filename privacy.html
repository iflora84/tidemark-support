Privacy Policy

Tidemark for iOS. Last updated 22 September 2026.

Tidemark collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer.

What stays on your device

The app stores your selected station, your favorite stations, your unit and clock preferences, and a cached copy of the tide predictions it has downloaded. All of it lives in the app's own container on your device and in iCloud device backups if you use them. None of it reaches the developer or any third party.

Location

Tidemark asks for location access only when you tap Find nearest stations in the station picker. The coordinate is used once, on your device, to sort the built-in station list by distance. It is not stored, not logged and not transmitted anywhere. If you never tap that button, the app never requests location. You can revoke access at any time in Settings, and the rest of the app continues to work.

Network requests

To show tide predictions the app requests them from the NOAA Center for Operational Oceanographic Products and Services at api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov. Each request contains the station ID you are viewing, a date range and an application identifier. It contains no personal identifier, no device identifier and no location. NOAA operates that service and its own privacy practices apply to it; see the NOAA privacy policy.

Children

Tidemark is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, any change would be a clarification rather than a new use of data.
